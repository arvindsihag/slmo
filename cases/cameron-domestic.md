# Cameron et al. — Domestic Robot Failure Taxonomy

**Domain:** Domestic HRI
**Source:** Cameron et al. (2024)

## Background

Four workshops with experts and potential users examined failure
scenarios involving a domestic dishwasher robot, and their
consequences for trustworthiness.

## Evidence

- Failure source and consequence matter for how trust is affected.
- Comprehensibility shapes how failures are judged.
- Different dimensions of trustworthiness are affected differently.

## SLMO encoding

    <evaluation / deployment planning,
     technical / social / service-provider,
     failure source + consequence + comprehensibility,
     trust reduction (differentiated)>

## Operational lesson

Not all failures reduce trust equally. A failure that users can
understand and that the system acknowledges may preserve trust better
than a silent or unexplained failure.

For practitioners: design failure communication, not only failure
prevention.
